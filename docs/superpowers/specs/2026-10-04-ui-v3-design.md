# NEXUS UI v3 design: paper and ink, pushed

Date: 4 October 2026. Status: approved direction (Design canvas "NEXUS v3 UI", boards Home, Product page, Home on a phone).

## 1. Goal

Make the shop stop looking generic without changing what it is. Same palette, same two typefaces, same pages, routes, nav labels and copy voice. What changes is composition: bigger display type, an offset hero with the product on a bench slab, big mono numbers instead of ruled tables, one bento and one ledger instead of three identical card rails, and a moderate motion layer. The real shop must match the approved boards at 1440 and at 390.

What Nick said: "I still feel like it looks a bit generic"; dials "design variance 9/10, motion 6/10, density 4/10"; brief "innovative tech marketplace, ecommerce"; "revert back to the previous one [colour] for now"; "ok it looks good lets continue with the build."

Assumptions (correct me if wrong): the back office, Radar and all data files stay untouched; the Trend Scout, cart drawer, checkout, orders, policies, setup, compare and collection pages keep their layouts and only inherit the token and component changes; no dark mode in this pass; product visuals stay the shop's own vector renders until photos exist.

Success: the home page and the MoGo 4 Laser page render as the boards do on desktop and phone; every existing test still passes; the taste pre-flight holds on the shipped pages (zero em or en dashes in UI copy, no three-equal-card row, one accent, one radius, hero of at most four text elements); the live site at sykunfts.github.io/nexus shows v3 after Nick pushes.

## 2. Dials and design read

Reading this as: redesign-overhaul of an e-commerce storefront for design-conscious Australian tech buyers, with a "checked, then shipped" trust-forward language, built on native CSS + Tailwind v4 + Motion (framer-motion, already a dependency), Bricolage Grotesque for display and body, Martian Mono for anything measured.

DESIGN_VARIANCE 9, MOTION_INTENSITY 6, VISUAL_DENSITY 4. The v2 site reads roughly 5 / 3 / 6, so the work is layout (asymmetry, offsets, empty zones), a fluid-CSS motion layer, and air.

From UI-UX Pro Max: the "feature-rich showcase" pattern with a brutalist execution (sharp corners, visible structure, asymmetric grid, stagger-in motion) is adopted. Its dark-and-gold palette and Cormorant serif are rejected: Nick asked to keep the palette, and the taste skill bans serif-by-default.

## 3. Scope

In scope:

- Tokens and global utilities in `src/styles/globals.css`.
- Shared components: `Button` sizes, `ProductCard`, `FlipBoard`, `Header`, `Footer` (in `App.tsx`).
- `Home` recomposed into seven sections (section 5).
- `ProductPage`: spec plate type and price, CTA pair, bench-results tiles, parts strip restyle (section 6).
- Bench data moved out of `Home.tsx` into `src/lib/bench.ts`.
- Dash policy in UI strings (section 8).
- Playwright screenshots regenerated and checked.

Out of scope: palette and fonts; routes, nav labels, slugs, anchor ids; catalogue data text (including en dashes inside spec values in `data.expansion.ts`, which are maker data, not UI copy); the Radar page; checkout, orders, policies, setup, compare, collection, guides layouts; dark mode; photos; swapping lucide for another icon set; the office.

## 4. Tokens, type and global rules

Colours are unchanged. Added to `@theme` and utilities in `globals.css`:

- `display-xl`: Bricolage, `opsz` 96, weight 500, letter-spacing -0.045em, line-height 0.94. Used once per page (home h1). Size is set per use: 108px at lg, 72px at md, 50px below.
- `display` (existing, hero and section titles): letter-spacing tightened to -0.035em, line-height 1.0. Section h2 sizes: 40px at lg, 30px below. Product h1: 64px at lg, 44px below.
- `numeral`: Martian Mono, weight 400, tabular figures, line-height 1. Used for the facts (34px lg / 24px phone), movers (26px / 22px), bench measurements (34px), product price (36px), ledger prices (17px).
- Radius: `--radius-ctl: 2px` stays the single radius. Every new surface (slabs, tiles, chips, buttons, segments) uses 2px. No pills.
- Spacing scale for density 4: section gap 104px at lg, 40px below; section padding-x stays `px-4 md:px-6`; container stays `max-w-[1440px]`.
- Shadows: only the hover lift, tinted to ink: `0 20px 34px -20px rgba(18,24,32,.4)`.
- `slab` utility: `background: var(--color-mat); color: var(--color-on-mat); border-radius: 2px`. The dark bench slab is a product backdrop, used on the hero and bento hero tile; it is not a section theme flip. The page stays light.
- Motion utilities: `.lift` (transform and shadow 250ms `--ease-snappy`, hover translateY(-3px), active scale .99); `.press` (active scale .98, 150ms). Mount reveals use framer-motion (section 9). The existing reduced-motion block keeps all of this at effectively zero.
- Buttons (`ui.tsx`): size `lg` becomes h-14 px-6 text-[17px]; new size `md` stays h-10 text-[14px]; `sm` h-8. `primary` stays ink on paper; `secondary` stays a 1px ink outline. Both get `press`.

Header: desktop bar 72px tall (`h-14` on phone), nav items 15px with 22px gap, search 220px wide in a white field, the "Trends" status text stays, Trend Scout stays an outline button at h-10, cart count in mono. No behaviour change (mega menu, hover intent, mobile drawer, keyboard).

Footer: same columns; the "All systems operational, status.nexus.store" line is removed (a decorative status strip pointing at a page that does not exist). The bottom strip keeps the company line, Privacy, Terms, Accessibility.

## 8. Copy and dash policy

- No em dash or en dash in UI strings. `etaText` returns "2-4 days" (hyphen); the same hyphen in `ConfirmedPage`, `OrdersPage` and the shipping-and-returns policy table. The empty comparison cell on the product specs tab and on the compare page reads "not listed" instead of "—". Catalogue values are left as they are.
- "The tagline's first clause" means `clause(tagline)` from `src/lib/text.ts`: the tagline up to (not including) its first comma, colon, semicolon or full stop. "A can-sized triple-laser 1080p projector with Google TV, a 2.5-hour battery..." gives "A can-sized triple-laser 1080p projector with Google TV".
- Hero subtext as in section 5. All other visible strings are existing strings.
- Verdict wording in section 6 replaces the square-only marks on the home table.

## 9. Motion (MOTION_INTENSITY 6)

- Mount reveal on the home hero (h1, then the left column, then the slab, 120ms apart) and on the product page (slab, then plate) using `motion.div` with `initial={{ opacity: 0, y: 18 }}`, 700ms, ease `[0.2, 0.8, 0.2, 1]`. Skipped entirely when `useReducedMotion()` is true (render at rest, no animation props).
- FlipBoard digits flip as today.
- `lift` on bento tiles, setup-row tiles and product cards; `press` on every Button and the ledger Add buttons.
- No scroll listeners, no marquee, no parallax.

## 10. Responsive rules

Breakpoints stay Tailwind's. Below `lg` (1024px) every multi-column section collapses to one column in the order written above; the hero h1 is 72px at md and 50px below; the bento becomes tile 1 plus a ledger; the facts are 2 by 2; the movers scroll; the setup panel precedes its row; the Sydney ledger drops the ETA column into the name cell. The slab overhang applies at lg only. Nothing scrolls horizontally except the rows that are designed to (movers and the setup row), which use `scroll-snap-type: x mandatory` and hide their scrollbar with the existing utility.

## 11. Accessibility

Contrast as today (ink on paper 15:1, ink-2 on paper 6.3:1, ink-3 on paper 5.1:1, on-mat on mat 13:1, ink on signal 5.4:1). Every clickable surface is a `button` or `a`; icon-only buttons keep their `aria-label`s; the hero figure link carries an `aria-label`; the status squares are `aria-hidden` and sit next to text that says the state; focus ring unchanged. Touch targets at least 44px on phone (Add buttons 44px, header controls 44px).
